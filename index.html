The reality of orphans in Pakistan

Children in danger every day

Orphans are abandoned, mistreated and forced to beg or work in inhumane conditions to survive, in a country where Christians are severely oppressed.

2%Christians, in a country that is 96% Muslim
8thcountry where Christians are most persecuted1
3rdin the world for child mortality2
11%of children aged 5 and over work3

Abandoned

Babies are rejected at birth, sometimes thrown in the garbage. Others are left alone on the streets, hungry and thirsty.

Forced labour and violence

Held captive in forced labour, in extreme heat of 113 °F. This boy carries 120 lb of bricks on his back, barefoot, and is struck every day.

Exploited

Captured to be exploited, they work with no way out and no chance to go to school.

Forced to beg

To survive, they must beg or search for food in the garbage. Exposed to violence, gangs and abuse.

They are our little brothers and sisters in the faith. They know violence, fear and abandonment.

With love and compassion, help us give them the food they need, a safe refuge, tenderness and the hope they so badly need.

Who we are

A mission born from a calling

In 2025, a few Christians in Canada answered the same call from God: to help 40 persecuted orphans in Pakistan, whom a Pakistani Christian had taken in and was feeding with all his strength, despite his own persecution.

That is how the non-profit organization Hands Together (Mains Ensemble in French) was born.

Our team is 100% volunteer. Your gifts go directly to support the orphans in Pakistan, through our two native missionaries. We are in touch with them every week and receive financial reports from them with receipts, photos and videos.

Our mission

To rescue and protect persecuted Christian orphans and children who are abandoned, mistreated or exploited, by giving them a safe home, food, education and the tools they need to build an independent future, in dignity and hope.

We also support the most vulnerable persecuted Christians and widows through humanitarian aid, tools and education that help them become independent and take charge of their lives.

Orphanage building project in Faisalabad

Let's build the Refuge of Hope

Today, the orphans in our care live in very precarious conditions:

  • Three to a small bed. In summer, they sleep outside.
  • Up to 122 °F in rooms with no windows or air conditioning.
  • No kitchen, no fridge, no clean water.
  • Threat of eviction: without a building, the orphanage is not legal. It has already happened.

We urgently need a suitable building. With no more room, we can no longer take in other orphans in danger and give them a safe refuge.

Our vision: build a refuge for at least 100 orphans

Over the years, hundreds of children will be rescued thanks to this refuge.

Nehemia's story

At 5 years old, Nehemia was rescued by our missionary. He had been abandoned on the street with his brother and sister, aged 2 and 3. He searched through garbage at a hospital entrance, living with hunger, thirst and fear.

Today Nehemia is 10. He is loved, fed and clothed. He is first in his class and received an award trophy.

Help us build the Refuge of Hope to house Nehemia and rescue other children like him.
